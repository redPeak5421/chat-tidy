# Chat Tidy Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 14, 2026

This policy covers the Chat Tidy Safari extension and its containing macOS application.

## Local processing

Chat Tidy operates on chatgpt.com, claude.ai and grok.com after you grant Safari website permission. To identify selected conversations and carry out your requested actions, the extension temporarily processes conversation titles, identifiers, workspace identifiers and the website session credentials required by that service. Grok's optional Show all feature reads conversation history metadata into page memory. Chat Tidy does not maintain a separate archive of your conversations.

Preferences, including language, enabled state, checkbox visibility and concurrency, and website cooldown timestamps are saved in local extension storage. They are not uploaded to a Chat Tidy server. Temporary conversation information is discarded when the page is unloaded. You can remove local extension data by uninstalling the extension; browser-managed data retention follows Safari's behavior.

## Network requests and third parties

User-requested operations go directly to the corresponding website using the existing signed-in session. Those services receive and process the requests under their own policies. Chat Tidy has no developer-operated server, analytics SDK, advertising SDK or tracking system, and does not sell personal data. It does not transmit conversation information to its developer.

Opening the GitHub project or support page is optional. GitHub processes visits and any information you choose to submit under its own privacy policy. Do not include private conversations, credentials or session tokens in public support issues.

## Your choices

You can disable Chat Tidy, revoke individual website permissions in Safari settings, or uninstall it. Batch deletion requires confirmation. Chat Tidy cannot restore deleted conversations; any recovery or retention provided by a website is controlled by that website. Stopping a batch prevents subsequent requests but cannot recall requests already sent.

## Contact and changes

For privacy questions, use https://github.com/redPeak5421/chat-tidy/issues without sharing sensitive information. This policy will be updated if the extension's data practices change.

# Chat Tidy 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 14 日

本政策适用于 Chat Tidy Safari 扩展及其 macOS 容器应用。

在你授权后，扩展仅在 chatgpt.com、claude.ai 和 grok.com 上工作。为识别所选会话并执行你的操作，扩展临时处理会话标题、标识符、工作区标识符及对应网站所需的登录凭据。Grok 的“展示全部”功能会把历史会话元数据加载到页面内存。Chat Tidy 不建立独立的聊天内容档案。

语言、启用状态、复选框显示方式、并发设置和网站冷却时间戳保存在扩展本地存储中。临时会话信息在页面卸载时丢弃。本地扩展数据可通过卸载扩展清理；浏览器管理的数据保留遵循 Safari 的行为。

操作请求使用现有登录会话直接发送给对应网站，网站按自身隐私政策处理。Chat Tidy 没有开发者服务器、统计分析 SDK、广告 SDK 或追踪系统，不出售个人数据，也不向开发者发送会话信息。

项目和支持链接指向 GitHub。是否访问由你决定；访问及主动提交的信息受 GitHub 隐私政策约束。请勿在公开问题中提供私人会话、密码或会话令牌。

你可禁用扩展、撤销网站权限或卸载扩展。批量删除需要确认，Chat Tidy 无法恢复删除的会话；网站的恢复或保留机制由网站决定。停止操作只能阻止后续请求，不能撤回已发送的请求。

隐私问题可通过 https://github.com/redPeak5421/chat-tidy/issues 联系，请勿公开敏感信息。如果数据处理方式改变，本政策将随之更新。
